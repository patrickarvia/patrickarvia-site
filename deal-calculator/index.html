Cairn Deal Calculator

A lightweight way to evaluate an enterprise opportunity using actual deal evidence rather than CRM stage or intuition alone.

Open calculator ↗ View GitHub ↗

What it is

A simple evidence check for complex enterprise deals.

Cairn Deal Calculator helps sellers inspect whether an opportunity has the conditions that usually matter in a complex buying process. It is designed as a quick diagnostic—not a replacement for judgment or a rigid qualification framework.

What it looks for

01

Executive sponsorship

Is there meaningful access to someone who can help the deal move internally?

02

Multithreading

Is the opportunity supported by more than one relationship or point of contact?

03

Commercial proof

Are budget, procurement, security, legal, and other buying requirements becoming concrete?

04

Next-step discipline

Is there a real, mutually understood action that moves the buying process forward?

Why it exists

Deal quality is easier to discuss when the evidence is visible.

Enterprise sales teams often debate whether an opportunity is “good” using stage, seller confidence, or generalized qualification language. The calculator turns that conversation toward specific evidence that can be observed, challenged, and improved.
