Cairn Pipeline Analyzer

Evidence-based pipeline analysis for testing whether CRM confidence is supported by what the buyer has actually done.

What it is

A sales pipeline tool that separates confidence from evidence.

Cairn Pipeline Analyzer is a free tool for enterprise sellers, sales leaders, and revenue teams who want to understand which opportunities are genuinely supported by buyer evidence and which are being carried by assumptions, stage labels, or seller optimism.

The problem

A CRM stage does not tell you whether a deal is real.

Enterprise opportunities often look healthy because the stage, close date, or seller narrative says they are. The analyzer looks for contradictions between that headline confidence and the underlying evidence.

That can include missing stakeholders, weak executive sponsorship, unclear next steps, stale activity, procurement uncertainty, or other gaps that make the forecast less reliable.

How it works

01

Review the pipeline

Start with the opportunities and the evidence currently attached to them.

02

Find contradictions

Identify where CRM confidence is not matched by buyer behavior or deal evidence.

03

Expose assumption gaps

Separate what is known from what the team is still assuming.

04

Prioritize proof

Surface the next evidence that would most improve confidence in the opportunity.

Built around a point of view

Forecasting is evidence management.

The tool comes from a broader idea I write about often: enterprise deals move through buyer commitments, internal consensus, commercial proof, and next-step evidence—not because a seller changed a CRM stage.
