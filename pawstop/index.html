Pawstop

A dog-friendly road trip planner built around the route you’re actually driving.

Try Pawstop ↗ View GitHub ↗

What it is

A route-aware way to find better breaks with a dog.

Pawstop is designed for people driving with dogs who want useful places to stop without relying on generic “near me” results or dirty highway rest areas. It looks at the route itself, then surfaces potential stops based on where they fit into the drive.

The problem

Nearby is not the same as useful.

A place can look close on a map and still be a bad road-trip stop because it sends you backward, creates a large detour, appears too early or too late in the drive, or has weak evidence that it will actually work for a dog.

Pawstop treats timing, direction, detour, dog access, and available place evidence as part of the same decision.

How it works

01

Start with a route

Plan around the trip you are already taking rather than a generic radius search.

02

Find route-aware stops

Discover candidate places positioned along the actual drive.

03

Evaluate detour and timing

Consider the practical cost of leaving the route and when the break would happen.

04

Use available evidence

Prefer stops with stronger signals that they are genuinely useful for travelers with dogs.

Why I built it

A small product built from a real travel problem.

I started Pawstop while thinking about long drives with my dog and how surprisingly difficult it is to find a good place to stop at the right point in a trip. The project became an experiment in routing, ranking, location discovery, evidence, and product design.
