About

Operator by background. Builder by curiosity.

I’ve spent my career working in complex enterprise sales across technology, data, logistics, and SaaS. The work has mostly been about helping large organizations make difficult decisions, building consensus across many stakeholders, and turning messy commercial processes into something more repeatable.

More recently, I’ve been turning some of those ideas into lightweight products and experiments of my own. I’m especially interested in where enterprise GTM, product, AI, and evidence-based decision making meet.
