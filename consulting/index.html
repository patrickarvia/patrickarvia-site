What it is

Practical GTM help for companies selling into complex organizations.

Cairn GTM is my independent sales and go-to-market advisory practice. It is designed for early-stage technology companies that need experienced help turning a promising product into a more repeatable enterprise revenue motion.

Areas of focus

01

Enterprise sales strategy

Account strategy, complex deal execution, stakeholder mapping, and commercial motion design.

02

Pipeline & forecasting

Evidence-based pipeline review, deal quality, forecast discipline, and operating cadence.

03

Outbound & messaging

Enterprise prospecting, positioning, playbooks, and repeatable outreach systems.

04

Practical AI adoption

Using AI and lightweight automation to make GTM work more useful without adding unnecessary complexity.

Who it is for

Early-stage teams building an enterprise motion.

The work is most relevant when a company has a strong product and some market signal, but enterprise selling still depends too heavily on founder instinct, individual heroics, inconsistent qualification, or a CRM process that does not capture what is really happening in the deal.

The perspective

Complex revenue becomes more manageable when the evidence gets clearer.

My work across enterprise SaaS, data, technology, and logistics shapes the approach: fewer generic frameworks, more attention to how decisions actually get made, how consensus forms, and what evidence tells you a deal is moving.
